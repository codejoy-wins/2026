Research coverage & how dates work

This snapshot combines your public website and JavaScript project lists, 12 monthly blog archives, prior project conversations, a saved Protocol 10 artifact, and all 185 public repositories returned by GitHub. The source archive includes tutorials, backups and possible duplicates. It is not a claim that you created 185 separate products.

“Recorded file commit” names a particular inspected file. It is not necessarily the last change to the entire project, and writing-page commits may be text or layout edits. Unknown dates stay unknown. “Repository pushed” is separate metadata. All displayed commit dates use America/Chicago. Later local edits and private repositories were unavailable.

Statuses and next steps are an editable first-pass assessment. Presentable does not mean fully tested. Unadopted suggestions are explicitly separated. Blog discoveries preserve headings as leads, not claims that each heading is a project. Duplicate/alias decisions, unidentified repositories and uncatalogued private work remain for review.

Stories and personal mythology are described as creative works. Their inclusion does not verify autobiographical, scientific or historical claims. External links need an internet connection; the tracker itself works offline.
