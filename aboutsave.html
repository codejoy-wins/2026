About the creator

Max Jann

I write software, songs, and stories. Sometimes I sign them Elam. This particular webpage is new and still under development, so bare with me.

Creator

I was born in Princeton, New Jersey, on September 30, 1991. My first car was a silver 2005 Mustang. I studied philosophy at UC Santa Cruz, learned full-stack development at Coding Dojo, and these days I work as an engineering technician at Applied Materials.

One thing my résumé leaves out is that I represented myself in a case against AMD and ZT Systems. I used AI as my legal associate. I lost. I was the one who had to stand in court and answer for the work. That experience still shapes how I use these tools, especially as they get stronger.

For a different introduction, play through one of my chess games or hear what my singing voice sounds like.

Website

I hand-coded maxjann.com before ChatGPT existed. I wanted a place where a song, a chess game, and a chapter of my book could all live under the same roof. Building that place became a project of its own.

I think of that original code as the bones. Working with AI, I’m giving it a nervous system. When I win a rapid game, my chess page can pick it up. When I return to a project, I can water it in Seed to Sky and see which ideas have gone a little dry. The site is starting to keep up with the person who built it.

Mythology

Interbeautiful begins with a man who wants to know whether someone else is better qualified to lead. He says he’s willing to surrender if he finds that person. Then he makes a recording and asks the woman he loves to help deliver it. It's about his theory of intelligence, centered around the acquisition of beauty, and his plan to donate himself in the most effective way possible.

First he uses profilaxis to extinguish any intial attempts to discredit him, then he argues that no one will agree that he is the right person for this project, but if they do blind testing, in various ways, even free response, and then do data analysis to see which candidate is the closest match, he is confident he will win. If he loses, he requests a meeting with who won, if he wins, he begins his donation his way.

His plan invloves memory erasure, to ensure authetic studies can be conducted. This is accomplished through the avoidence of novel experiences, and continuing all communication through the medium of the recordings.

His plan is to harness the power of individuals like himself in order to help the tribe come to power. To go from individual manifestation, to group manifestation, forming a team of people working together, as the good guys, making the world a better place.

He has since surrendered to Christ, and would defer to him in all things.

Some scenes come back to me with the texture of memory. I call them pseudomemories. I can leave the question of what happened open, go to work, come home, and have fun writing the scene the way I remember it. In a screenplay I get to ask where she was sitting, what she heard in his voice, and whether she wanted to say yes.

Her part is equal to his, and he only did any of this because he wanted to do what she wanted to do, and she said she wanted to do what he wanted to do. He wanted to be destroyed, and to be used as a battery for mankind. Transforming the chemical reaction of true love into the electrical energy of this website.

Someday I want to make the film. Before you meet either of them, I want these words on the screen.

“Based on a false memory.”

If the memory is false, can the story still be true? I’d like you to have your own answer by the time the credits roll.
