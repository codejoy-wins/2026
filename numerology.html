About the mathematics & interpretation

Gematria assigns numeric values to letters and adds them to calculate a word or phrase. “Full Reduction” reduces each letter's ordinal value to 1–9, rather than reducing the final sum. “Reverse” assigns Z=1 through A=26. Different ciphers are different mathematical systems, not independent confirmation of a prediction.

This independent Jann Software implementation uses traditional and publicly documented arithmetic rules. It is not affiliated with Gematrinator. Results should be treated as symbolic and recreational exploration, not scientific evidence of hidden causation or future events.

Letters A–Z are counted; spaces and punctuation are ignored. Accented Latin letters are normalized to their base letters. Numbers entered as digits are ignored rather than spelled out. This version does not include Hebrew/Greek scripts, Gematrinator's proprietary databases, account features, or all of its specialized ciphers.
